## POV: Hugo
- Hugo hauls four small PHCUs home, wires them to a power strip to run a vanity crypto hash experiment, tiny status LEDs winking to show they’re on, then forgets them under the desk.
- Victoria arrives for a hookup; they joke about her boyfriend (Hugo knows who he is, reader doesn’t).
- During sex everything is intense—Victoria leaves him (and her) tired, satisfied, and already wanting more; in the afterglow a new hum/pressure blooms in Hugo’s head—he blames perfume/beer.
- Days blur: he skips class, binge-learns French in days, texts Victoria in French at 2 a.m.
- Obsession escalates: he covers walls with relativity diagrams, writes tensors on the bathroom mirror, neglects freelance gigs and anyone trying to reach him.
- Victoria drifts in and out, alternating between hookups and judgment; Hugo jokes he’s “obsessed.”
- Practical life decays: fridge empty, microwave meals, rent unpaid; eviction notice snaps him out.
- Packing to leave, he rediscovers the inert, LED-blinking PHCUs and powers them down as part of shutting the apartment. Afterward, the absence hits like coming up for air—relief mixed with a hollow, abandoned feeling, and he realizes the relentless drive came from them.
