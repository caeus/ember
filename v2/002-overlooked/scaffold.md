## POV: Clement
1. Night on campus/city: Clement sits on library steps with an almost-cold cheap coffee, scrolling his phone, watching couples and girls drift past with a sour, bitter eye.
2. A woman <!-- (Victoria, but he doesn’t know her name) --> in a rushy, restless mood clocks him as convenient and invisible; she presses in close with casual confidence.
3. She pulls him into a side hallway/alcove; the encounter is urgent, messy, risky—his first real intimacy, her way to scratch an itch he can’t perceive.
4. Clement reads the moment as proof he was right: he didn’t change, the world just finally noticed. He’s shocked and exhilarated, buzzing with “I’m not ugly, I’m not awkward.”
5. She leaves without looking back, disappearing into the crowd; he stands there, dazed, replaying every second, convinced it means more than it does. <!-- offscreen: a nascent dreambond ignites through the encounter. -->
6. He rushes online to tell his usual crowd (servers/feeds/DMs); some don’t believe him, others believe and call her a slut and worse.
7. He stews on their reactions: bitter they doubt him, and uneasy that their insults don’t fit how he feels—if anything, he’s grateful. He is determined to find her again.
