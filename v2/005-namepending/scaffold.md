## POV: Imogen
1. Imogen attends Nimrod’s funeral to continue her investigation; she watches family and friends with quiet intent.
2. She speaks with his mother and relatives to map his recent spiral; they mention his ex-girlfriend Andrea, who left him but still came to pay respects.
3. As a couple arrives—a plump, short girl with straight hair and big black eyes, and a stocky short guy barely taller than Imogen—Andrea drifts away as if pushed. Imogen freezes: the girl matches one of her own imagined characters to an eerie degree (a natural do-gooder, though Imogen’s details are TBD). She’s instantly suspicious of the guy, too.
4. Imogen edges closer and gently draws them into conversation; both are visibly shaken.
