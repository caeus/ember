## 1. Core premise

A phenomenon — later understood as **B2BI / the Field** — begins interfering with human
brain activity.

Its first impossible-to-ignore manifestation is a small but statistically disturbing group
of people who enter prolonged **stuporous states**, lasting up to roughly three months.

- Externally: lethargic, vegetative, unreachable.
- Neurologically: highly active, broadly REM-like. The mismatch is the scandal.

These are the **original sleepers**. Imogen is one of them.

The original sleepers eventually **wake nearly simultaneously**.

One to two weeks *before* they wake, the **replicas** begin: further events, each affecting
a different group of people.

### Events are internally synchronous

This is the phenomenon's one hard, repeatable characteristic:

> **Within a single event, the affected go under at nearly the same time and wake at nearly
> the same time.** Every event, including the original.

**Duration varies between events, never within them.** The original ran to roughly three
months. Replicas run shorter, by differing amounts, with no derivable relationship between
an event's duration and anything measurable about it or the people it took.

So an event is a **cohort**: a set of people who share nothing except the hours they went
under and the hours they came back. That shared timing is real, observable, and the reason
the phenomenon eventually becomes undeniable — it is what turns scattered weird cases into
something institutions are forced to acknowledge and count (see §5).

It is also the limit of what synchrony gives anyone. Events can be identified, numbered and
bounded. **Cohort membership cannot be explained.** Why these people and not others resists
every model anyone builds, and must keep resisting.

The original sleepers develop hunches and perceptual disturbances associated with these
events — they become **crude detectors without understanding what they detect**.

The sleepers are the most *visible* manifestation, not the only one. Many other people are
affected in other ways, most of them never classified as cases.

## 2. The Field

The Field interferes directly with brain activity. **Transmission is semantic, not
sensory.**

It does **not** transmit:

- sounds
- images
- spoken language
- touch
- literal dream scenery
- any other sensory representation

A receiving brain nevertheless *experiences* something — warmth, voices, rooms, smells,
pressure, spaces — because **rendering is the recipient's own work**.

> Something equivalent to "mother's love" may arrive as warmth, as a childhood room, as
> pressure against the body, as a smell, or as something with no obvious relation to any
> of those. The rendering says more about the recipient than about the content.

### The rendering rule (hard)

Two people receiving related Field content must **not** conveniently report the same dream
architecture, images, phrases or figures. If two renderings resemble each other, that
resemblance must be explicable by something the two people share (shared childhood,
shared language, shared trauma) — and even then it should be partial and disputable.

The phenomenon stays **broad, noisy, hard to measure, and resistant to a clean superpower
taxonomy**. There is no power list. There is no reliable test. There is no moment where it
resolves into a system.

---

## 3. Mystery discipline (hard rules)

Discoveries are expensive. They are paid for with accumulated noisy observation.

Early observations should often be **useless, local, ambiguous, misclassified, or
explainable away**. Institutions will invent **wrong categories and wrong models** and act
on them before reality gets clearer. Wrong models are not filler — they are plot.

**Banned moves:**

- treating the synchrony of §1 as *progress*. The timing is a fact of the world and people
  will notice it — that is how the phenomenon gets acknowledged at all. What is banned is
  anyone converting *they went under in the same hour* into a step toward the mechanism.
  Synchrony says **that** and **when**. It never says **how** or **why these people**.
- sleepers describing identical experiences
- correlations that are obvious on sight
- a single anomaly pointing at the mechanism
- implausibly correct deductions from tiny samples
- any character arriving at the truth faster than the evidence permits

**Explicitly discarded** (previous generation, too cheap): common dream geography,
convenient shared architecture, obvious synchronized sleeper signals, fast sleeper-family
discoveries, easy recognition of Field correlations.

Mystery should **expand before it contracts**.

## 4. Characters

### Imogen — original sleeper

We **will** show scenes from her POV while she sleeps. She is unknowingly
exploring/interacting with the Field.

During this period she bonds with two entities:

| | **Near** | **Witness** |
|---|---|---|
| distance | close | more distant |
| manner | soothing | attentive, observational |
| relation | emotionally bonded to Imogen | lingering, registering |

Both can interact with entities and things that Imogen otherwise experiences as alien or
inert, and can guide her through experiences she could not otherwise survive or parse.

Any apparent place, body, colour or voice in these scenes is **Imogen's brain rendering a
semantic interaction**. It is not transmitted scenery and must not be treated as a
location that another character could ever visit or corroborate.

These Field scenes are **one thread among many**. Not every generation is Imogen
exploring.

### Hugo — revealed late

Hugo arrives **late** and his arrival must **recontextualize a large body of material the
reader has already absorbed**. Do not introduce him early as "the guy behind everything."

- His unrelated experiment caused the original sleeper event.
- The experiment was **not** about consciousness, minds or the Field. `[OPEN]` mundane
  domain: cryptography / software-AI experimentation / home automation / thesis project /
  a game. Exact mechanism `[OPEN]`.
- **The first run was an accident.** He did not decide to do it; it fired. He logged
  nothing at the time and reconstructed the entry long afterwards from memory, so the one
  event he most needs to account for is the one he has the least data on. He initially does
  not know he caused anything.
- **Every event after the first is his.** There is no other source. He repeats and modifies
  the experiment to establish statistical relevance — which is to say, to talk himself out
  of being the cause.

  This is the book's central obscenity and it should be stated without decoration: **each
  attempt to clear himself produces another cohort.** He is running an argument in which
  other people are the sample. The more rigorous he is, the more people go under.

- **Duration is uptime.** An event lasts exactly as long as the units run.
  **The first event lasted three months because he forgot it.** The person closest to him
  went under; he spent those months caretaking at their bedside; the rig sat powered in an
  unvisited flat the whole time. The duration of the original event is the length of his
  vigil. His care for the victim is what sustained the harm, and he was never in the room
  where the cause was running.

  Replicas are short because he runs those deliberately and briefly. So the variable isn't
  a parameter he tunes — it's a power strip, and the first and largest reading of it is a
  man's grief.

  It also explains §1's synchrony for free: a cohort goes under together because something
  switched on, and wakes together because something switched off. The one hard repeatable
  characteristic of the phenomenon is a man's hand.

  Consequence: **the original sleepers wake because he clears the flat.** He stopped paying
  rent while caretaking and is given a deadline — no confrontation, no villain, just a date.
  He unplugs the units himself, as part of moving out, and attaches no meaning to it (G008).

  Consequence: researchers chasing a duration rule are modelling a man's housing situation.

- `[PROPOSED]` He has **no instrumentation**. He learns what his runs did the same way
  everyone else does: from reports. An experimenter reading his results in the news.
- **He is closely related to one of the original sleepers.** `[OPEN: who — sibling,
  parent, oldest friend, ex]` This is the reason he connects anything to anything. He was
  not watching a phenomenon, he was watching one bed. Nobody else had a reason to correlate
  a private hobby with a stranger's medical event; he had a reason to correlate it with
  theirs.

- **The relief that licensed everything.** When he ran it a second time, that person did
  not go under. For a long while he took this as proof of coincidence — and it is why he
  ran it so many more times. The relief is monstrous in retrospect and must not be written
  as monstrous at the time: other people went under during that run. He was happy anyway.

- **That person is taken by the first event**, which is why he was caretaking, which is
  why he forgot the rig, which is why it ran for three months. The loop is closed and
  airless: the experiment took them, and attending to them is what kept the experiment
  running.

- **Eventually that person goes under again**, in a later run, and his coincidence defence
  dies with it (G009).

- `[PROPOSED — timing wrinkle]` §1 says replicas begin one to two weeks before the
  originals wake, but a man who has forgotten the rig cannot be running experiments.
  Resolution: in the last week or two he returns to the flat to pack, rediscovers the units,
  and **handles** them — unplugging, moving, repowering. Those accidental disturbances are
  the first replicas. His deliberate runs (G007 onward) come after the waking. It keeps the
  first replicas accidental, like run 1.

- **Hugo is affected, but never a sleeper.** What it does to him has no name and no
  symptoms anyone would record: the world becomes **more alive and more dull at the same
  time**. Keep that formulation — the contradiction is the symptom, and he has nothing to
  compare it against, so for a long time he does not count it as anything.

- He is curious, secretive, and **increasingly culpable**.
- He may approach sleepers or their families under a plausible pretext, wanting firsthand
  observation without disclosing involvement.

### Victoria — vector, not victim

Victoria is **genuinely highly sexual and adventurous before B2BI**. The Field does not
make her sexual. Her sexuality stays **agentic and is never pathologized**.

Unknown to Victoria, and initially to the reader: **sexual contact with her can prime or
sensitize people to the Field.** Partners carry an increased probability of later
manifestation.

`[OPEN, non-sacred]` Victoria eventually realizes she may have altered people without their
knowledge or consent. The moral weight must land as:

> "I may have changed people without their knowing."

and **never** as:

> "My promiscuity was immoral."

### Clement - the mysogynistic redpill who falls for Victoria

Clement and Victoria are important intersecting paths. Inherited material has him strongly
redpill and ideologically rigid, with Victoria becoming deeply important to him while she
remains sexually nonexclusive. **His worldview gets stressed and broken** — Victoria is not
domesticated into it.

**Do not assume Clement's actions or interior in any scene that is not his POV.** The same
restriction applies to Victoria and Hugo.

## 6a. The date

**26 October 2031.** The day the original cohort wakes.

It becomes the date the world organizes itself around — the one everyone can place
themselves on, the anniversary, the shorthand, probably the name `[OPEN: does the folk term
come from the date? "the 26th" is sitting right there]`. The book should let it accumulate
that weight ordinarily, by repetition, the way real dates acquire it.

**It is the deadline on Hugo's eviction notice.** *Vacate by 26 October 2031.* He clears
the flat, unplugs the units, and the cohort surfaces — on a date chosen by a letting agent
filling in a form.

**Reveal.** The notice is shown, with the date on it, in G008 — positioned after row 8.
No commentary, no character noticing, nothing after it. The reader has spent the book with
that date and meets it here as a line of administrative boilerplate.

**Calendar discipline.** For that to land, the book must be **sparing with explicit dates
everywhere else.** If the reader is given dates routinely, the notice is just another one.
Most scenes should locate themselves by season, weather, and how long since — so that the
few hard dates the book does print are all load-bearing.

`[OPEN]` Onset of the original event: roughly three months earlier, late July 2031. Exact
date undecided — and the temptation to make it the 26th of July is probably too neat.

## 7. Further mechanics

Not scenes — constraints. These bind what any scene may do.

**Rendering is autobiographical.** What a person renders Field content *as* comes from
their own material. A rendering that could be swapped into another character's scene
without loss is wrong. The reader learns about people while learning nothing about the
Field; that asymmetry is deliberate.

**Detection is somatic, not informational.** What woken sleepers get during replica events
is dread, nausea, a mood arriving before its reason — never content. They cannot tell a hit
from a bad day. A sleeper may never confirm an event. At most they are later found to have
been upset on the right day, which is not proof and must never be played as proof.

**The Field is not restricted to humans.** Animals are affected. Nobody has a framework for
what they observe, and it must stay that way — the moment an animal event becomes legible
evidence, it has been spent badly.

**Event duration resists rule-finding — but an event's internal timing does not.** Per §1,
a cohort is synchronous; what varies is how long one event runs versus another, and that
variance correlates with nothing measurable. Taxonomies built on duration survive one more
event and collapse. The duration is noise. The synchrony is signal, and the only one.

**Temporal grouping is real; spatial grouping is an artifact.** These are different things
and the book must not let them blur:

| | what it is | what it yields |
|---|---|---|
| **cohort** — same event, same hours | genuine, observable, countable | the fact that forces acknowledgment |
| **cluster** — same ward, same city | administrative: one catchment, one hospital that took the overflow | nothing |

A reader should be able to look at a room full of sleepers and correctly conclude nothing
from the room. The same reader should be able to look at a *timestamp* and correctly
conclude that something happened. Holds under either answer to the §5 geography question —
a cohort may be scattered across a continent and is still a cohort.

**Who is in a cohort stays unexplained.** Synchrony tells anyone *that* and *when*, never
*why these people*. Every model of membership is a wrong model.

**Hugo is never the agent of the largest turns.** Every major state change in the
phenomenon is decided by something outside him:

| turn | who decides |
|---|---|
| the first event begins | nobody — it fires by accident |
| the first event ends; the cohort wakes | an eviction deadline. His hand pulls the plug; the date was set by someone else |
| the first replicas | his own handling while packing, unintended |

What he *chooses* are the middle runs — the deliberate ones, which matter least and damn
him most. **His agency and his culpability are misaligned**, and that misalignment is the
reason the book can hold him without becoming his.

This is load-bearing against canon §4's instruction that he arrives late and
recontextualizes. A character who decides the big turns is a protagonist no matter how
late he appears. A character things happen *through* is not.

Forward constraint: whatever the book's resolution turns out to be, **Hugo does not get to
decide it either.** `[OPEN: what does]`

---

**Hugo never articulates a theory.** He has observations, correlations and dread, never a
model. He is the one person positioned to deliver exposition, which is exactly why he must
not. No Hugo scene contains a sentence of the form *"so it must be that—"*.

## 8. Texture

**Interference in the wave sense.** Superposition — two sources producing a pattern neither
contains, bands of varying width. Composition, not disruption. It gives B2BI's wrong name
an accidental rightness: not one brain reaching another, but overlapping contributions
producing something no contributor holds. No character may draw the analogy out loud and be
right; if someone says it, they are reaching.

**Compound-word register.** Prototyped in `stash/Hugos_first_dream.md` — jammed compounds
(*softsharp*, *friendlydisappointment*, *needneed*) that deliver meaning before it resolves
into image or sentence. The closest available match for "meaning arrives whole, scenery gets
assembled late". If used, it is **Imogen-specific** — a property of her brain, not a house
style for the Field. Another sleeper's POV needs a different solution or §2's rendering rule
breaks at the level of prose.

---

## 9. Convergence

Imogen, Victoria, Clement and Hugo's paths **eventually intersect**. Do not force the
intersections early, and do not invent a character's decision merely to make an
intersection happen.
