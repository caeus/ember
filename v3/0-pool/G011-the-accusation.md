**Logline.** The spouse of a prominent journalist blames Victoria for the change in him, and makes the accusation public rather than face what she cannot explain.

**Beats**

1. At a press event, the journalist displays a new coldness his spouse barely recognizes; colleagues call it professional courage.
2. She finds evidence of his liaison with Victoria. The timing feels like proof, though it is not.
3. Offered a chance to speak privately, she instead takes the microphone and names Victoria.
4. The accusation lands. Her partner does not defend her or Victoria; the silence makes her more certain.

**Notes.** A public crisis and an intimate betrayal in one scene. No diagnosis or supernatural proof. Victoria is observed only, never given invented motives or interiority. Consequences can ripple through politics and reporting without turning this into a morality tale about her sexuality.
