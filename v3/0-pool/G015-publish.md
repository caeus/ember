**Logline.** Hugo releases the instructions for his experiments anonymously, hoping disclosure will relieve guilt he cannot confess.

**Beats**

1. He rereads a report of a death and opens the files describing the apparatus and run procedure.
2. He types a warning that says what he knows, then removes the sentences that would identify his responsibility.
3. He tells himself others will scrutinize it, prevent mistakes, or share the burden; he cannot bear to sign his name.
4. He posts the archive. Its availability is irreversible even if nobody ever succeeds in using it.

**Notes.** Operational documentation is not a theory of the Field: Hugo never explains the mechanism. No copycat cohort is established. Reproduction by others would require a user-approved change to canon §4.
