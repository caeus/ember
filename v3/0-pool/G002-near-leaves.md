**Logline.** Near thins out, and Imogen chooses being watched over being alone — a real
choice, made for reasons she could state if she had language.

**Beats**

1. Establish that there is no geography here, and no Imogen-shaped body to have a position
   in it. Her sense of Near is rendered as something like proximity without location —
   a certainty, not a place. The render is **hers alone** and must not be reusable by any
   other character.
2. Near does not leave, because there is no away. Near *thins*. The certainty she didn't
   know she was holding loosens by degrees.
3. She tries to reproduce Near by remembering it, and finds that remembering produces a
   hollow version — a shape with no answer in it. This is the first thing she learns here,
   and it is useless to her.
4. What remains presses in: things that are not hostile, only **unaddressed to her**.
   Render them differently from anything rendered earlier in the scene — the variance is
   the point.
5. Witness is still there. The attention that never closes the distance. She has been
   avoiding it on the grounds that being looked at is worse than being alone.
6. She revises that. Not nobly — the way you revise a position under load.
7. She turns toward Witness. Witness does not approach and does not soothe. It
   **registers** her. Something in her settles, slightly, and she dislikes that it settles.
8. Nothing is understood. She has traded one bond's shape for another's, and knows — in the
   wordless way available to her — that she has made a decision she cannot undo from in
   here.

**Notes**

- Advances the Field's mechanics by *demonstration*: content arrives, rendering is personal,
  comprehension doesn't follow.
- Keeps Near and Witness characterized by **manner**, never by appearance. Resist giving
  either a face, a voice or a name she hears.
- REL: this is the scene that makes Witness matter later. The bond is chosen, not received,
  which should cost her something after she wakes.
- Risk flag: this genome is the easiest in the pool to overwrite into lyrical mush. If
  promoted, the draft needs hard concrete verbs and short sentences.
