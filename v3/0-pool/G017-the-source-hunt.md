**Logline.** A bereaved sister mistakes a technical clue in leaked blueprints for the identity of the culprit, and makes an accusation she cannot retract.

**Beats**

1. She brings a journalist the anonymous files and a name derived from a reused handle in the archive.
2. The journalist says the attribution is weak. She hears another person asking her to accept uncertainty about her brother's death.
3. She posts the name and workplace herself, against the journalist's advice.
4. The first angry responses arrive. She feels relief so strong she mistakes it for certainty.

**Notes.** The lead may be entirely wrong; the scene must not reveal Hugo by deduction. The choice makes innocent collateral damage possible without certifying a new source of the cohorts.
