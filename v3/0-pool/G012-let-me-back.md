**Logline.** A recovered sleeper refuses the life her family welcomes her back into and walks away to find whoever could return her to the sleep.

**Beats**

1. In a discharge meeting, she tells her husband and doctor she wants to go back. Nobody understands that she means it.
2. The doctor cannot offer that procedure. Her husband speaks of the months they lost; to her, they are asking her to surrender the only life that felt whole.
3. Ashamed by their relief, she signs herself out against advice and refuses the ride home.
4. She leaves carrying a printed list of alleged sources and cures, knowing most are frauds.

**Notes.** Her desire can be genuine without making the Field a paradise. This creates a search driven by loss, not a clue-solving shortcut. No method of reentry is established.
