**Logline.** A partner finds the next devastating corruption dossier in the hands of someone who seems unable to care that it will ruin their life.

**Beats**

1. She recognizes the formatting of the notorious anonymous leaks on her partner's open laptop.
2. She begs him not to publish. He reacts to the risk of prison, and to her distress, with the same absent politeness.
3. She offers to destroy the draft and pretend she saw nothing. He posts it instead, with a composure that frightens her more than anger would.
4. She leaves the room without reporting him, and hates herself for the relief.

**Notes.** This is a different human hand, not proof of a transferred entity. The source may be acting voluntarily, under orders, or through something neither of them understands. Its mechanism stays sealed.
