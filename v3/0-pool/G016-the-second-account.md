**Logline.** An investigator arrests the whistleblower threatening the presidency, only to watch the same campaign continue while the detainee is isolated.

**Beats**

1. She brings a stack of corroborated leaks to an interview with a person who appears to have no politics, friends or recognizable investment in any of it.
2. The detainee can identify neither a motive nor the people whose careers the posts have destroyed.
3. Another dossier appears online while the suspect is cuffed and disconnected. A colleague says, plausibly, that there must be accomplices.
4. She records "organized network" in her report, because she needs a category that permits the investigation to continue.

**Notes.** The corruption can reach the president, without naming a country yet. An emotionally vacant suspect is not proof of Field agency; the handoff can still be a mundane conspiracy. No author-only physics is revealed.
