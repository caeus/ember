**Logline.** Clement intervenes when his friends harass a woman; the recording captures a hero he refuses to believe he is.

**Beats**

1. At a gathering, his friends corner a woman with the rhetoric he normally repeats.
2. Their mockery becomes physical. He steps between them, angry partly because they are making him look like them.
3. A friend films him taking the woman's side. He could retreat and save face, but doubles down.
4. Someone uploads the clip before he can force an explanation. He drafts a furious denial and cannot make himself send it.

**Notes.** This does not make Clement enlightened. He can protect someone while preserving an ugly theory about women. The wider hero/traitor backlash belongs to later scenes, not a montage here.
