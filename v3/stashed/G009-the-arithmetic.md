**Logline.** Hugo had stopped checking on them, because they'd been spared once.

**Beats**

1. A run in progress, compressed to almost nothing — by now it's three lines of routine.
   The scene opens with him not thinking about it.
2. **He has stopped watching them.** This is the cruelty and it should be stated plainly:
   the relief of G007 hardened into an assumption. They were spared once, so he stopped
   checking. His attention went back to the data.
3. The news reaches him through the ordinary channel, because he is still the contact —
   the administrative intimacy from G007 beat 1, doing its work. Someone phones him about
   a person he had stopped thinking of as at risk.
4. He does the arithmetic without wanting to. It matches. `[No theory, no model — canon §7.
   Two timestamps and a subtraction. That's all he is ever allowed.]`
5. **What breaks is the argument, not the conscience.** Precision matters here: he does not
   discover he is guilty — he has suspected that for a long time. He loses the only
   sentence that was holding the suspicion off. The scene should make the reader notice
   that the loss he registers first is *rhetorical*.
6. His first impulse is something small, practical and damning `[OPEN: what — checking the
   notebook before going to them is the obvious candidate and may be too obvious]`.
7. He goes to them. End in the ward, him sitting down — **in the chair where, in G001, a
   sister sat.** Not stated. Just the same chair.

**Notes**

- Closes the loop canon §4 now carries: the first event took them, caretaking them is what
  left the rig running for three months, and here the thing he restarted takes them a
  second time. He is the only person who could have protected them and the only reason
  they need protecting.
- **Against G007.** G007 is relief at a sample of one. G009 is the bill for it. The two
  scenes are the same mistake viewed from either end and should be written to rhyme —
  same watching, same arithmetic, opposite answer.
- **Do not let him decide anything here.** No resolution, no confession, no stopping. The
  scene ends with a man sitting down. What he does next belongs to a later genome, and
  the honest version is probably that he runs it again.
