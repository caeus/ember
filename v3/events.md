# Candidate canonical events

**None of these is canon.** These are possible *story-history* disruptions, not scene
assets or new world rules. The user alone can accept a candidate into `canon.md`.
`favored` records enthusiasm, not a binding decision; `deferred` means do not develop
further without renewed interest. Each candidate names its causal pressure and the
first single-scene genome that tests it.

## Constraints and open debts

- **E001:** No instant proof that Victoria caused the journalist's change, and no moral
  punishment of her sexual agency. The spouse's belief can be mistaken.
- **E002:** A sleeper's wish to return does not imply anyone can actually send them back.
  Searching for a source must not shortcut Hugo's late reveal.
- **E004:** Neither a containment mechanism nor the entity's dangerousness is settled.
  The scene can show a desperate choice, not verify a monster.
- **E005/E006:** The number and causes of deaths remain uncommitted; `canon.md` §4 still
  assigns every sleeper cohort to Hugo alone. A publicly reproducible machine would
  require changing that rule, not slipping in a second source.
- **E007:** The source's apparent hollowness and succession are observations, not a
  demonstration of possession or probability steering. The corruption may be genuine
  without anyone knowing how the records were obtained.
- **E008:** Keep in reserve; do not quietly introduce the child through a different genome.
