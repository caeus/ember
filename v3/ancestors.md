# Ancestors — pre-reset genetic material

**Not canon.** These predate the reset. They are useful genes, not commitments. Anything
drawn from here must be mutated to obey `canon.md` — semantic-not-sensory transmission, the
rendering rule, and the stricter ban on cheap discovery. Several of these originally relied
on exactly the shared-experience shortcuts that are now forbidden.

## Previously favoured

- **G5-A / G5-C crossover** — strongly favoured.
- **Later relationship variants G6-B, G6-D, G6-F** — strongly favoured.

## Motifs with traction

| motif | gene | mutation required |
|---|---|---|
| **The Quiet Ward** | Imogen, hospital, a cluster of sleepers; Hugo much later | The cluster must not read as a correlation anyone can act on. Proximity between sleepers should be bureaucratic (one ward, one catchment) and explain nothing. |
| **Murmuration** | manifestations coupling nonhuman/animal | Strong — nothing about canon restricts the Field to humans, and animals produce observations no one can interpret. Keep it uninterpretable. |
| **The Borrowed Day** | missing consciousness, replica-related phenomena | Fits the replicas directly. Durations vary by person; the variance should frustrate classification rather than hint at a rule. |
| **Dead Signal** | semantic leakage involving absent or dead people; severing | Dangerous. Under the rendering rule, a "dead person" in the Field is a rendering, and the scene must never confirm otherwise. Best used as a **wrong model** institutions or families adopt. |
| **The Choir Treaty** | Victoria-related social and relationship consequences | Must survive the §4 guardrail: consequences for people around her, never a verdict on her sexuality. |

## Discarded lineage

## v2-era material (`/v2`, `/stash`, `/characters`)

A different continuity: PHCUs as physical cause, named dreambonds (Ember, Silhouette,
Embrace), Hugo as early protagonist, the units as an on/off switch for the whole
phenomenon. **Structurally incompatible with v3** — v3's Hugo is late, unaware, and
culpable rather than central.

Still mineable for:

- prose voice (`v2/001-obsession/dist.md`, `v2/002-overlooked/dist.md`)
- Clement's interiority before Victoria (`v2/002-overlooked`)
- the Gale conversation as a template for *institutional dismissal*
  (`stash/hugo_visits_gale.md`) — note it contains the line about a signal "buried under
  layers of interference, filtering and noise", which is where B2BI's vocabulary can
  plausibly come from
- `stash/Hugos_first_dream.md` as a register experiment for rendering non-sensory content
  in compound language — the technique transfers to Imogen even though the content doesn't
